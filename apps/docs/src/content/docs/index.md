---
title: DICOM Viewer
description: View local DICOM studies in a React app.
---

Add a browser-based DICOM viewer to your React app. Open individual images or scroll through a series in 2D. Switch compatible CT and CBCT series to linked cross-sections and a rotatable volume view.

[Read the quickstart](./quickstart/)

<a href="/dicom-viewer/demo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open demo (new tab)</a>

Try a synthetic dental radiograph, a 48-slice head CT, or a lossless JPEG 2000 sample. You can also open your own files. The viewer reads them locally in your browser.
